Some of the most meaningful chapters of our lives happen *in between* — between who we were and who we are becoming.

In this talk and book presentation, we gather to explore those in-between spaces: migration, loss, reinvention, and the courage it takes to begin again. Through stories and shared reflection, we'll look at how our identities are shaped across cultures and seasons, and how every ending quietly carries the seed of a new beginning.

**What to expect:** a warm, intimate conversation, readings from the book, and space for questions and connection.
